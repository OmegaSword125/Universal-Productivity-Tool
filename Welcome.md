# Make room for your thoughts

Welcome to **UPT 1.0 Beta**, your local Markdown workspace.

Write on the left, read on the right. Your notes are ordinary `.md` files you own.

## Start small

1. Create a note with **Ctrl+N**.
2. Write something worth keeping.
3. Press **Ctrl+S** and choose a filename.

Keep related notes in folders. Press **F5** after changing files outside UPT.

## A little structure

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Ctrl+S | Save your note |
| Ctrl+F | Find a word |
| Ctrl+E | Show or hide preview |

```python
ideas = ["read", "think", "write"]
for idea in ideas:
    print(idea)
```

> Your words stay on your computer. No accounts. No telemetry.
